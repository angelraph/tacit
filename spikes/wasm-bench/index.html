Tacit prover

Real Zcash Ironwood proofs, made in your browser.

Tacit runs sealed-bid auctions on Zcash. Every bid is a fully shielded deposit, so each bidder's own device has to produce a zero-knowledge proof for it. This page does that work: it builds the Ironwood (post NU6.3) proving key, then creates the same proofs a bid and a small settlement need.

Nothing leaves your machine. On a 4 core laptop, the key takes about 18 s and a bid proof about 20 s.

Press run. Keep this tab in front: browsers slow down hidden tabs a lot.

Part of Tacit, a Zecathon entry: sealed bids timelocked with drand, 2 of 3 FROST vaults, and a public viewing key so anyone can audit an auction without learning who took part.
